Trip Highlights

  • Experience the thrill of a lifetime with our Coral Island Excursion. Board a speedboat and glide across turquoise waters to soft, sandy beaches. Spend your day swimming, relaxing, or trying exciting water sports like parasailing and jet skiing. This tropical paradise is the highlight of any Pattaya holiday.

  • Immerse yourself in Bangkok's rich cultural heritage with guided temple tours. Visit the magnificent Golden Buddha Temple (Wat Traimit) and the stunning Marble Temple (Wat Benchamabophit). Explore the Grand Palace complex and witness the spiritual heart of Thailand through ancient architecture and traditions.

  • Witness the world-famous Alcazar Cabaret Show in Pattaya, featuring spectacular performances by talented artists in dazzling costumes. This optional evening entertainment showcases Thailand's vibrant culture through music, dance, and theatrical performances that will leave you mesmerized.

  • Enjoy a romantic dinner cruise along the Chao Phraya River, Bangkok's lifeline. Savor delicious Thai cuisine while admiring illuminated temples and modern skyscrapers from the water. This optional luxury experience offers stunning city views and creates unforgettable memories of your Bangkok nights.

  • Perfect Visa-Free Holiday starting at just ₹15,000 per person.

Trip Summary

About the Tour:

From the picturesque white sand beaches like Railay to the ancient relics, this Thailand vacation blends nightlife, culture, cuisine, and water sports for a must‑visit experience. Wander through famed temples, stroll Chinatown’s vibrant streets, relax on palm‑fringed shores, or chase sunsets at Promthep Cape.

Quick info:

Destinations Covered: Bangkok, Phuket, Krabi
Duration: 8 Days, 7 Nights
Start Point: Bangkok International Airport
End Point: Krabi International Airport

Inclusions:

  • Accommodation: 2 Nights in Pattaya, 2 Nights in Bangkok
  • Meals: Breakfast as per the itinerary
  • Transfers: Airport pick up/drop + intercity transfers on a private basis
  • Visa assistance: Provided (charges extra)
  • Flights: To & Fro flights from Delhi to Bangkok and Krabi to Delhi

Not to Miss in Bangkok:

  • Visit the Grand Palace
  • Unwind at Lumphini Park
  • Explore Chinatown’s lively streets and cuisine
  • Watch the sunset from Promthep Cape in Phuket
  • Try bamboo rafting, trekking to waterfalls, and more at Khao Sok National Park

How To Reach?

Bangkok is well connected internationally via Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang Airports. There are direct flights from major Indian cities like Ahmedabad, Delhi, Mumbai, and Kolkata; typical flight time is about 5 hours.
